20 videos and slideshows a day

Every day you get 20 ready-to-post videos and slideshows for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and Facebook, tailored to your product. Swipe right to approve, and HeyCatch writes the caption, picks the best time and publishes.

Replies and posts on Reddit, X and Threads

Every day HeyCatch finds the conversations where your buyers already talk about the problem you solve, and drafts replies and posts in your voice, written for each platform and community. You review, then post.

A new blog article every day (SEO & GEO)

One article a day, written for search engines and AI assistants in your market’s language and built on your product research. Copy it to your site in one click.

Marketing fixes for your site and app listing

HeyCatch audits your landing page and your App Store or Google Play listing for positioning, messaging and conversion blockers, then gives you a ranked plan of fixes. Copy them all as one prompt into Lovable, Cursor or your AI builder.

Technical SEO and GEO fixes for your site

Our crawler runs 68 checks across up to 300 pages of your site, from titles and meta to indexing and structured data, and turns them into a ranked list of fixes. GEO fixes help ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude read and quote your site.

Account health for every platform

Reddit, X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook and Threads: HeyCatch reads dozens of signals from each connected account, matched to how that platform ranks accounts. You see how much you can safely post today and what to fix first.

Analytics: site, product and social

Install analytics on your landing page, web app or mobile app with one prompt. Track traffic sources, build funnels from first visit to payment, follow every user’s journey with an AI summary, and see how every post performs.

Frequently asked questions

HeyCatch is an AI-powered growth platform built for builders, solo founders, and small teams shipping SaaS and consumer apps. It operates on proven playbooks and knows which moves actually work at your stage. HeyCatch analyzes your product, audience, and competitors, sets up your analytics, and runs daily marketing moves across your whole funnel: landing page, app store, onboarding, emails, SEO and GEO, distribution, and customer discovery. As results come in, it tracks what's working and adapts the plan, so it evolves with your traction. HeyCatch replaces the early marketing hire for founders going from zero to their first 100 paying users and $1k MRR without paid ads.

HeyCatch is built for bootstrapping builders going from zero to their first 100 paying users. That includes indie hackers, solo founders, vibe coders, and small teams shipping SaaS and consumer apps, especially those who launched on Lovable, Bolt, Cursor, or Replit. It is not built for funded growth teams, agencies, or enterprise marketing departments.

HeyCatch turns a product audit, audience research, and market insights into a daily roadmap of marketing moves that it runs for you across most channels. It starts by auditing your product page and running ICP, competitor, and niche research, then generates the roadmap and ships daily moves across landing-page changes, SEO and GEO content, emails, and distribution setup. For example, a SaaS tool built on Lovable might get three SEO articles published, a Reddit distribution plan with approved drafts, X build-in-public content, a landing-page messaging rewrite, and a high-converting pricing page set up in the first week.
